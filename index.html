Our games.

A few ideas we’d love to play, from strange little planets to circuits that bend gravity.

Exploration platformer

Orbit Oddity

Hop between floating islands as a little explorer with very big boots. Every planet has its own gravity, plants that react to your jumps, and hidden paths that reward a detour.

Arcade racer

Violet Overdrive

Throw an anti-gravity pod around tight corners and charge your boost with each clean drift. Take the high route for speed or cut through the city to find riskier shortcuts.

Puzzle adventure

Moss & Ember

Carry the last ember through a forest of sleeping ruins. Change the color of your lantern to wake different plants and piece together paths that disappear when the light moves.
